Pipes draws the actual pipe dream: each empty cell is a pair of elbows and each filled cell is a crossing. Two pipes cross at most once; where they meet again, the filled cell acts as elbows, so pipe i leaves through column w(i).

Choose a cell with an empty square immediately to its right. Look upward through these two columns, skipping rows where both squares are filled. If the first remaining row has both squares empty, move the cell into its right square. A row with only one filled square blocks the move. After moving a cell, you can choose to create a cell in its original position. A small circle at the top right of a cell means it can be moved.
